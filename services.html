Emergency AC Repair

Houston heat doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we. Our emergency line is answered by a live dispatcher 24 hours a day, and our trucks carry the parts that fail most.

  • Same-day diagnosis with flat-rate pricing approved before work begins
  • Stocked vans for compressors, capacitors, contactors, blower motors, and control boards
  • Honest triage: if a repair buys you years, we'll say so — if it's time to replace, we'll show you the math
hvac

AC Installation & Replacement

A new system is a 15-year decision. We size it with a proper Manual J load calculation, price it in writing, and install it to manufacturer spec so your warranty actually means something.

  • High-efficiency systems from 14.3 SEER2 up to variable-speed inverter units
  • Permits, inspections, and old-equipment haul-away included — no surprise line items
  • Written workmanship guarantee alongside the manufacturer warranty
mode_heat

Heating & Furnace Service

Furnaces, heat pumps, and air handlers repaired and safety-checked. Every gas-furnace visit includes a carbon monoxide safety check at no extra charge.

  • Ignition, blower, and heat-exchanger repairs on all major brands
  • Heat pump diagnosis, repair, and changeover service
  • Pre-season safety checks before the first cold front
air

Indoor Air Quality

Gulf Coast air is humid, allergen-heavy, and hard on filters. We build systems that actually manage it.

  • Whole-home dehumidification and ventilation balancing
  • High-MERV media filters and whole-home purification
  • Humidity, particle, and airflow readings taken on every tune-up
ducts

Ductwork & Airflow

Hot rooms and weak airflow are usually duct problems, not equipment problems. We measure before we recommend.

  • Room-by-room airflow and static-pressure diagnostics
  • Duct sealing, insulation, and rebalancing
  • Reroutes and returns for renovated floor plans

  • Walk-in cooler and freezer refrigeration support
  • After-hours service windows for busy locations
