Seventy years on the name on the truck

The story of a one-truck refrigeration shop that became Houston's family HVAC company.

Built by one family. Run the same way.

In 1952, our founder opened a one-man refrigeration shop in a garage off Washington Avenue, fixing ice boxes and window units for returning servicemen and their young families. The rule was simple from day one: do the work you'd put your own name on, and charge a fair price for it.

Three generations later, the fleet is bigger and the equipment is smarter, but the rule hasn't moved. We don't use commission salespeople. We don't quote one price and invoice another. We don't let the phone ring out at 2 a.m. in August.

That's also why our customers' kids call us for their own homes — 300+ verified reviews later, we're still Houston's family HVAC company.
